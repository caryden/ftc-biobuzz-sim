Four numbers to start with

-150points per match when a launcher is 3 times less repeatable. Nothing else that we tested comes close.
-82points for an alliance that runs no AUTO. Two solo routines that ignore each other lose 52.
73%of TELEOP is driving, and 5% is launching. A shorter trip buys more than a faster shooter.
+6+2.7points for every kilogrampound that you leave off the robot. Top speed bought nothing.
